# Marketing Strategy

## Priority

1. Australia B1: largest proven Bali volume and clear paid-B1 need.
2. Malaysia purpose-specific B2B/C/D/E: high national volume but free A1 weakens tourist-B1 demand.
3. China localized B1 and business: meaningful/growing Bali demand but requires language, channel, and payment readiness.

## Funnel

Use eligibility content or a checker before lead capture. Segment by country, purpose, arrival window, stay length, and group size. Show government fee, service fee, inclusions, exclusions, refund terms, and private-service disclaimer before payment. Use consented reminders for arrival declarations and extension deadlines.

## Experiment guardrail

Create one hypothesis per test. Define primary metric as post-ad contribution per eligible lead or per paid customer. Track eligibility rate, qualified-lead rate, payment conversion, CAC, fulfilment cost, error/rework, refund/chargeback, customer response time, extension attach, and contribution after acquisition.

Start with small country-specific pilots. Do not extrapolate the supplied `$6 CPL`, `30% conversion`, `+25% conversion`, or `40% extension need`; all are assumptions.

## Profit strategy

Grow profit through correct segmentation, lower rework, transparent premium service, B2B coordination, repeat-entry products, and eligible extensions. Avoid unnecessary-visa sales, hidden fees, unsupported urgency, and discounts that ignore full variable cost.
