# Customer Qualification

Ask only what is necessary at each stage:

1. Nationality and passport type.
2. Current country and whether already in Indonesia.
3. Exact purpose, including any hands-on work or Indonesian compensation.
4. Arrival date, intended length, and number of entries.
5. Individual, family, or company group; number of travellers.
6. Need for extension, sponsor, invoice, or urgent handling.
7. Passport validity and return/onward travel readiness.

Do not collect passport images or bank statements during initial qualification. After identifying the likely route, disclose private-service status, provide fee separation, obtain consent, and move documents to an approved secure channel.

Escalate when purpose is mixed, work-like, regulated, journalistic, religious, investment/employment-related, the nationality is uncertain, the traveller has overstayed, a prior rejection exists, or the deadline is too close for a safe claim.
