# Package Recommendation

## Routing logic

- Malaysian ordinary-passport leisure/MICE visit up to 30 days: explain A1 Rp0 first; do not default to B1.
- Eligible Australian or Chinese leisure/family visit up to 30 days: compare direct B1 Rp500k with private assistance transparently.
- Eligible B1 traveller staying 31–60 days: discuss B1 plus one extension and its deadline.
- Business meetings, negotiation, purchase, or goods inspection: check B1 permission and C2 fit based on purpose, duration, and entry pattern.
- Repeated business travel: evaluate D2.
- Audit/QC, trial work, after-sales, installation, internship, employment, investment, or family stay: use the specific C/D/E route and escalate for compliance review.

## Offer rule

Never recommend a more expensive route solely to improve margin. Recommend the lowest-complexity lawful route that fits the customer's facts, then sell assistance through review quality, coordination, support, and reduced administrative friction.

If no Visa Kita price is `active`, present a non-binding estimate or request a quotation; label government fees separately and date the estimate.
