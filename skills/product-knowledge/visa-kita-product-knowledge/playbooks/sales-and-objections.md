# Sales and Objections

## Why not apply directly?

`You can apply directly through Indonesian Immigration and pay the official fee. Visa Kita is a private optional service for route guidance, document review, submission assistance, and support. We will show the government and service charges separately.`

## Can you guarantee approval or entry?

`No. The immigration authority decides the visa and the officer at the border decides entry. We can help reduce avoidable application errors, but cannot guarantee either decision.`

## Why does a Malaysian traveller need a visa?

`For many Malaysian ordinary-passport visits of up to 30 days, the A1 visa-exemption route may be available at no visa fee and cannot be extended. We first check purpose and stay length; a paid route may be relevant for longer, repeat-entry, employment, or specific business activities.`

## Is the Bali levy included?

State the package's approved inclusion. Never confuse the separate Rp150,000 Bali tourist levy with the free All Indonesia arrival declaration.

## How fast is it?

`We can state our document-review target if approved, but the authority controls visa processing and entry. Apply early and do not rely on an unapproved fixed turnaround.`

## Is my passport safe?

Explain the actual approved collection channel, access, retention, deletion, and incident-contact policy. If these controls are not documented, do not claim encryption or safety guarantees.
