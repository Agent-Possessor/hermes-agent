# Product Overview

Visa Kita is a proposed private Indonesian visa-assistance service operated under PT Excitech Teknologi Indonesia. It is not the Directorate General of Immigration and no official-partner status is documented in the supplied sources.

## Customer problem

Customers need help selecting a lawful visa route, preparing accurate documents, paying applicable charges, tracking the application, and planning arrival or extension steps. The service value is assistance and risk reduction; it is not the sale of approval or entry.

## Product families

- Arrival products: A1 visa exemption and B1 e-VOA guidance.
- Single-entry visits: C-series for business and specific activities.
- Multiple-entry visits: D-series.
- Temporary stay: E-series for work, investment, and family.
- Post-arrival services: eligible extensions and arrival-compliance guidance.
- B2B: group intake, consolidated administration, invoice, and account handling.

## Positioning

Lead with correct route selection, document review, transparent fee separation, human support, and secure handling. Do not compete only on speed: government processing and entry decisions are outside Visa Kita's control.
