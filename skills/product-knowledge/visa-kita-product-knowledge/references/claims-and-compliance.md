# Claims and Compliance

## Safe claims

- Describe Visa Kita as a private assistance service.
- Separate official government fees, third-party costs, and Visa Kita service fees.
- Say that the team can review documents, submit or assist with submission, and provide status support only when those operations are confirmed.
- State official durations and fees with source date and a re-verification caveat.

## Prohibited or unverified claims

Do not publish the following without operational evidence and approval:

- `official partner`, `guaranteed approval`, `guaranteed entry`, or `official e-VOA QR` as if Visa Kita issued it;
- `60 minutes`, `3 hours`, `24 hours`, or any fixed processing SLA;
- `1,200+ travellers assisted`, `24/7 support`, `encrypted data`, or specific conversion lifts;
- active PayPal, UnionPay, Russian-card, eSIM, passport-pickup, group-dashboard, or dedicated-manager support;
- `skip the airport queue` or autogate eligibility for every traveller;
- advice that permits paid work under a visit visa.

## Privacy minimum

Passport images, dates of birth, financial statements, travel records, and contact details are personal data. Apply purpose limitation, consent or another lawful basis, least-data collection, access control, retention/deletion rules, incident handling, and vendor review. Indonesia's Law No. 27 of 2022 governs personal-data processing. Do not collect documents through ad lead forms or ordinary chat unless the channel and policy are approved.

## Marketing disclaimer

Use: `Visa Kita is a private visa-assistance service and is not the Indonesian Directorate General of Immigration. Government decisions, processing times, and entry remain under the relevant authorities.`
