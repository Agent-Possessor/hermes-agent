# Master Index

Use this file as the quickest entry point into Visa Kita product knowledge.

## Reading order

1. `references/product-overview.md` for identity, scope, and positioning.
2. `references/product-catalog.md` for visa routes, boundaries, and official-fee context.
3. `pricing/price-book.md` for commercial prices and package status.
4. `pricing/packages-and-addons.md` for included benefits and add-ons.
5. `pricing/unit-economics.md` for margin structure and pricing logic.
6. `pricing/competitor-benchmark.md` for market pricing context.
7. `data/*.csv` for raw numeric tables and assumptions.
8. `playbooks/customer-qualification.md` and `playbooks/package-recommendation.md` for decisioning.
9. `playbooks/marketing-strategy.md` and `playbooks/sales-and-objections.md` for growth and customer-facing copy.
10. `markets/australia.md`, `markets/china.md`, and `markets/malaysia.md` for market-specific strategy.
11. `references/claims-and-compliance.md` and `references/source-register.md` for safe claims and source freshness.

## What lives where

- Product definition and boundaries live in `references/`.
- Commercial packaging and pricing live in `pricing/`.
- Numeric working tables live in `data/`.
- Customer decision flows live in `playbooks/`.
- Market strategy lives in `markets/`.

## Usage rule

If a question mixes route selection, pricing, and marketing, resolve it in this order:

1. Confirm eligibility and visa route.
2. Check official fee and internal offer status.
3. Check margin and competitor context.
4. Check market fit and channel implications.
5. Only then craft the answer or recommendation.
