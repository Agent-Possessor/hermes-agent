## B1 operational facts

- Passport validity must be at least six months at entry; a return/onward ticket may be required.
- B1 is single entry. Every traveller, including children, needs the applicable permission.
- The same passport used in the application must be used to enter.
- Approval does not guarantee entry; the immigration officer decides admission.
- All international arrivals must use the free All Indonesia declaration, available up to three days before arrival.
- Bali's foreign-tourist levy is separate from visa/VOA and is Rp150,000 where applicable.

## Qualification warning

Nationality alone is insufficient. Verify passport type, purpose, duration, entry count, location, sponsor need, and whether the traveller will receive compensation in Indonesia.
