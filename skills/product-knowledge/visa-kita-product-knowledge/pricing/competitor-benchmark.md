## Implications

- A Rp950,000 Saver is Rp150,000 above Flado and Rp220,000 above VFS; `levy guidance` alone is weak differentiation because the declaration and official information are available online.
- A Rp1,250,000 Fast Track requires validated premium fulfilment. It cannot rely on an unverified 60-minute promise.
- The draft Rp950,000 extension is above Flado standard but below Visas-Indonesia's range; it needs a clear service-area and custody model.
- Excitech's C2 Rp4m sits between Flado standard and priority. Its D2 one-year price Rp6m is slightly below Flado's Rp6.5m; two-year Rp8.5m is below Rp9.5m; five-year Rp12m is materially below Rp15.5m. Confirm cost and official-fee changes before positioning.
