# Unit Economics

## Definitions

```text
payment fee = price × payment rate
variable cost = government + levy + payment fee + partner/benefit + CS + operations
contribution before acquisition = price - variable cost
CPA = CPL / lead-to-paid conversion rate
contribution after acquisition = contribution before acquisition - CPA
post-ad margin = contribution after acquisition / price
break-even conversion = CPL / contribution before acquisition
maximum CPL = contribution before acquisition × target conversion
```

Use `contribution`, not `profit bersih`, unless fixed overhead, tax, refunds, rework, chargebacks, and owner costs are included.

## Decision guardrails

- Set a target post-ad contribution and margin before launch.
- Include refund, rejection-support, chargeback, and rework reserves once measured.
- Report results by country, product, purpose, and channel; blended CPL hides bad cohorts.
- Evaluate add-ons using attach rate and incremental contribution, not headline revenue.
- Do not claim that one B2B close equals a fixed number of B2C customers without the same cost basis.
