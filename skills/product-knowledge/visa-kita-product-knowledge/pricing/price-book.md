## B1 Visa on Arrival (VOA/e-VOA) pricing

These are private assistance packages for the B1 electronic Visa on Arrival, not separate visa types. A customer may also apply directly through Indonesian Immigration at the official B1 fee. Separate government fees, Visa Kita service value, and optional third-party benefits when presenting an offer.
