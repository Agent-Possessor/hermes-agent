# Packages and Add-ons

## Benefit states

- `confirmed`: documented and operational.
- `partner-dependent`: sell only after partner and fulfilment are confirmed.
- `planned`: concept, not customer-facing.
- `unverified`: appears in copy but lacks evidence.
- `retired`: do not use.

| Benefit | State | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Application/document review | planned | Define checklist, liability boundary, and handoff |
| Status and WhatsApp support | planned | Publish staffed hours, not `24/7` |
| Bali levy guidance | planned | Clearly separate the free All Indonesia declaration and paid Bali levy |
| Visa Kita pays Bali levy | unverified | Confirm workflow, exemption handling, refund, and receipt ownership |
| 1GB eSIM | partner-dependent | Confirm device eligibility, coverage, activation, cost, and privacy |
| Express / urgent processing | unverified | Do not promise government decision time |
| Company invoice | planned | Confirm entity, tax treatment, and invoice workflow |
| PayPal / UnionPay / Russian cards | unverified | Enable and test before publishing |
| Passport pickup | unverified | Requires secure custody, chain-of-custody, insurance, and service area |
| Group dashboard / bulk upload | unverified | Requires product and privacy validation |
| Dedicated manager | unverified | Requires staffing and response standard |
| Extension consultation | planned | Consultation is not approval or eligibility guarantee |
| Digital Bali arrival guide | planned | Low-cost bonus; keep official information current |

## Packaging principle

Bundle benefits only when they reduce a validated customer friction. Price optional third-party items as explicit add-ons when attach rate or willingness-to-pay is unknown. Never hide government or levy charges inside a vague `service fee`.
