# Australia Market

## Evidence

- Australia contributed 23.44% of Bali's 6,948,754 direct foreign arrivals in 2025 and remained above 20% in every published month from January through May 2026.
- Internet penetration was 97.1% and social-media identities equalled 77.7% of the population in late 2025.
- The Reserve Bank of Australia's 2025 survey says cards remain the most-used consumer payment method.
- Australian passport holders are eligible for B1 subject to current rules.

## Strategy

Prioritize Australia first for B1 acquisition. Lead with transparent all-in breakdown, accurate document review, card payment, responsive English support, and arrival-readiness. Use Google Search for high-intent terms and Meta/Instagram for retargeting; test click-to-WhatsApp versus web checkout rather than assuming WhatsApp preference.

## Segments

- Short leisure trip under 30 days: compare direct B1 with assisted B1 honestly.
- Stay over 30 days: sell extension planning and deadline reminders.
- Family/group: individual eligibility plus coordinated intake.
- Meeting/purchasing: check whether B1 purpose fits or C2 is more appropriate.

## Tests

Test `human document check`, `transparent total cost`, and `extension-ready support` as separate propositions. Measure paid conversion, post-ad contribution, refund/chargeback rate, error/rework rate, and extension attach rate. Do not launch `60-minute` creative without measured operational evidence.
