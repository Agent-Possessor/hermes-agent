# China Market

## Evidence

- China accounted for 8.56% of direct Bali arrivals in July 2025, second after Australia; February 2026 arrivals from China rose 98.54% year on year.
- China is an eligible B1 nationality subject to current rules.
- China had 1.83 billion cellular connections in late 2025; the local digital ecosystem is mobile-first and differs from Western channels.
- A China–Indonesia QR interoperability pilot began in September 2025, allowing invited Chinese users to use UnionPay app or Alipay at Indonesian QRIS merchants. This does not prove Visa Kita can accept those methods online.
- Flado already exposes Chinese-language content, so localization is a competitive baseline.

## Strategy

Treat China as a localization and payment-readiness project, not an English Meta campaign. Build Simplified Chinese landing and support content, WeChat/Weixin contact capability, China-access testing, and a verified payment path before paid acquisition. Evaluate Xiaohongshu/RED for travel discovery, Douyin for video reach, and Baidu or travel partners for intent; validate channel access and local advertising requirements.

## Offer angle

Emphasize Chinese-language assistance, passport-data accuracy, payment clarity, arrival checklist, and family/group coordination. eSIM is secondary unless device compatibility and activation are proven.

## Gate before launch

Require a native-language copy review, staffed response process, test transactions from mainland-issued payment instruments, privacy notice in Chinese, and a pilot with manually reviewed leads. Report B1 and C2/business cohorts separately.
