# Malaysia Market

## Evidence

- Malaysia frequently ranked first nationally in 2025 foreign-arrival shares, including 17.3% in November.
- Malaysian ordinary-passport travellers are eligible for A1 visa exemption: Rp0, up to 30 days, non-extendable.
- Malaysia's internet penetration was 98.0% in late 2025. Facebook ad reach was 23.0 million, Instagram 16.1 million, and TikTok's reported adult ad reach was very large but carries measurement caveats.

## Strategic correction

Do not target ordinary Malaysian leisure visitors under 30 days with a paid B1 offer by default. First explain the free A1 route. Selling unnecessary B1 assistance would damage trust and increase refunds.

## Valuable segments

- Travellers who need more than 30 days and understand A1 cannot be extended.
- Repeat business travellers needing D2 or a purpose-specific route.
- Corporate groups, MICE, audit/QC, installation, after-sales, or employment cases.
- Family and investor temporary-stay cases.

## Strategy

Use English and Bahasa Melayu variants. Test Google Search for purpose-specific demand, LinkedIn for corporate/HR/admin decision-makers, and Meta/TikTok only with eligibility-first lead forms. Route customer support through WhatsApp if operationally staffed. Position on correct route selection, corporate invoice, document coordination, and repeat-entry planning—not generic `get your VOA` advertising.
