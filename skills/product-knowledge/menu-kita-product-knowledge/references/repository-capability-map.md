# Repository-Derived Capability Map

## `menu-kita-referral-ally`
**Purpose:** Use when a Menu Kita owner asks about referral, ally, affiliate, partner program, word-of-mouth growth, ambassador, community collaboration, or local partnership ideas.

**When relevant:** - Owner asks for referral/ally program. - Owner wants more customers from community, offices, gym, school, events, or local partners. - Owner asks how to reward customers or partners for bringing buyers. - Owner asks about affiliate/ally growth.

## `menu-kita-finance-reporting`
**Purpose:** Use when a Menu Kita owner asks for daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, custom-period, or all-time finance reports including balance sheet, income statement, worksheet, journal details, or export-ready summaries.

## `menu-kita-marketing-guide`
**Purpose:** Start here for Menu Kita marketing assistance. Use when a merchant owner asks how to increase restaurant sales, or when an admin/project owner asks how to increase app users, merchant acquisition, onboarding activation, or platform growth.

## `menu-kita-platform-growth`
**Purpose:** Use when a Menu Kita admin, project owner, or platform owner asks how to grow the Menu Kita platform, increase merchant users, increase customer users, improve activation, or prioritize growth roadmap.

**When relevant:** - Admin/project owner asks how to increase platform users. - Team asks how to acquire more merchants. - Team asks how to increase customer ordering through Menu Kita. - Team asks what growth experiment to prioritize. - Team asks for GTM, launch, funnel, activation, or growth roadmap.
