# FAQ

## What is Dompet-Ku?
Dompet-Ku is a self-custody digital asset wallet designed to help users manage supported crypto assets, review wallet actions, and access supported Web3 services from one experience.

## Does Dompet-Ku hold my money for me?
Dompet-Ku is positioned as self-custody. You remain responsible for protecting access to your wallet and recovery information.

## Can Dompet-Ku recover my seed phrase?
No. A recovery phrase must remain private. Do not share it with anyone, including support or an AI assistant.

## Is Dompet-Ku free?
The repository describes the wallet as free to use, but blockchain network fees and third-party service costs may apply. Confirm current pricing in the live product before quoting exact costs.

## Which networks are supported?
The repository references EVM-compatible networks, Solana, and Bitcoin. Supported networks can vary by release, so users should check the current network selector or official product information.

## Why do I need to review a transaction?
Blockchain transactions can be irreversible. Reviewing the destination, asset, amount, network, and fee helps prevent mistakes and reduces exposure to suspicious requests.

## What is a token approval?
A token approval gives a dApp or contract permission to use a specified amount of a token. Approve only when you understand why it is required and prefer limited allowances when appropriate.

## Can I use Dompet-Ku with dApps?
Dompet-Ku is designed to support dApp connection and wallet-request review. Only connect to sites you trust and review each request separately.

## What is a bridge?
A bridge is a way to move an asset or its representation between blockchain networks. It may involve fees, timing differences, changing quotes, and third-party risk.

## Can the AI make decisions for me?
No. Assistant features should help explain options and risks, but the user must make the final decision and confirm every transaction independently.
