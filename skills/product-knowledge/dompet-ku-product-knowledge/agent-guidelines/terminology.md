# Plain-Language Terminology

- **Self-custody:** You control access to your wallet; you are responsible for protecting its recovery information.
- **Recovery phrase / seed phrase:** A secret backup phrase that can restore wallet access. Never share it.
- **Private key:** A secret credential that can authorize wallet actions. Never share it.
- **Network / chain:** The blockchain environment an asset or transaction uses.
- **Token:** A digital asset on a blockchain.
- **Gas / network fee:** The cost charged by a blockchain network to process an action.
- **dApp:** A decentralized application that can request a wallet connection, signature, approval, or transaction.
- **Signature:** A wallet confirmation that proves the wallet owner approved a message or action; it can have important consequences.
- **Approval / allowance:** Permission granted to a contract or dApp to use a token up to a chosen limit.
- **NFT:** A unique digital asset or collectible represented on a blockchain.
- **Bridge:** A process used to move assets or representations between blockchain networks.
- **Slippage:** The possible difference between an expected and final exchange or bridge amount.
- **Transaction hash:** A public identifier used to look up a blockchain transaction.
