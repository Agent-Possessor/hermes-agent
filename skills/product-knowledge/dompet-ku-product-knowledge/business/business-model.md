# Business Model and Commercial Principles

## Product economics overview
Dompet-Ku can be offered as a free-to-download self-custody wallet while generating revenue from optional, user-initiated services. The repository indicates wallet, bridge, payment, market, and AI-related product directions; commercial activation must be validated against live contracts, legal requirements, and user disclosures.

## Potential revenue streams
### 1. Cross-chain routing or bridge service fee
A transparent fee may be charged when a user intentionally uses a supported cross-network route. The fee must be shown before confirmation and separated from network or third-party costs where possible.

### 2. Swap or routing referral revenue
Where token exchange or routing is supported, Dompet-Ku may receive a referral or partner fee. Any fee or commercial relationship should be disclosed clearly.

### 3. Fiat or payment partner revenue
If payment, on-ramp, off-ramp, or merchant-related flows are enabled through partners, revenue may come from service fees or revenue share. Availability will depend on jurisdiction, partner coverage, and compliance.

### 4. Premium assistance or advanced tools
Optional paid features may include enhanced portfolio insights, advanced risk explanations, premium notifications, or usage-based AI assistance. These must not be positioned as financial advice or guaranteed profit tools.

### 5. Ecosystem partnerships
Partnerships may include dApps, networks, NFT projects, merchant programs, or educational campaigns. Commercial promotion must remain distinguishable from objective risk information.

## Commercial rules
- Fees must be visible before user confirmation.
- Do not hide fees inside vague “best rate” language.
- Never monetize by requesting seed phrases, private keys, or direct custody of user assets.
- Avoid promoting a route merely because it has a higher commercial payout.
- Present any sponsored or partner placement transparently.
- Treat compliance, consumer protection, tax, and jurisdictional requirements as release gates for regulated flows.

## Pricing status
No definitive public pricing schedule should be claimed from this repository alone. Use “network fees and third-party costs may apply” unless current pricing has been confirmed.
