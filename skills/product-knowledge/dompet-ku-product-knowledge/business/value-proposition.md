# Value Proposition

## For individual users
- One product experience for supported multi-chain assets.
- Easier receiving, sending, tracking, and reviewing wallet activity.
- Better visibility before signing, approving, or moving assets.
- Saved addresses and QR flows to reduce input friction.
- A path toward contextual help for users who are less familiar with Web3.

## For active Web3 users
- More convenient access to supported dApps and wallet requests.
- Centralized awareness of transaction details, approvals, and assets.
- Cross-network movement flows designed to make complex actions easier to review.

## For NFT users
- A combined place to view supported tokens and collectibles.
- Clearer transfer workflows alongside the main wallet experience.

## For ecosystem partners
- A user-facing wallet channel for supported network, dApp, payment, or routing experiences.
- Potential education and onboarding surface for users entering Web3.

## For the Dompet-Ku business
- A foundation for optional service revenue without taking custody of user assets.
- A platform for long-term ecosystem partnerships and trusted user engagement.
- Product differentiation through multi-chain simplicity, transaction-awareness, and assistant-led education.

## Differentiation narrative
Dompet-Ku should not compete only on asset storage. Its stronger narrative is:

**“A practical multi-chain wallet that helps users understand and review what they are doing before they act.”**
