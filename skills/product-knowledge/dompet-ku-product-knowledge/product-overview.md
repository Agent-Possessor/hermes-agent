# Product Overview — Dompet-Ku

## What Dompet-Ku is
Dompet-Ku is a digital asset wallet for people who want to hold, receive, send, monitor, and use crypto assets across multiple blockchain ecosystems without relying on a separate wallet for each ecosystem.

The product is positioned as **self-custody**: the user remains responsible for access to their wallet and must protect their recovery information. Dompet-Ku should be described as a tool that helps users manage and approve actions, not as a bank, investment manager, or guarantee provider.

## Problem it addresses
Crypto users often face a fragmented experience:
- Different networks may require different wallets.
- Addresses, token balances, and transaction histories are hard to monitor in one place.
- dApp approvals and token permissions can be confusing or risky.
- Moving assets between networks can involve multiple steps and unexpected costs.
- New users may feel intimidated by unfamiliar terms and irreversible transactions.

Dompet-Ku aims to reduce this complexity through a single product experience for asset management, Web3 access, transaction review, and cross-network movement.

## Primary audiences
### 1. Crypto newcomers
People entering Web3 who need a guided, less intimidating experience for receiving, sending, and understanding assets.

### 2. Everyday multi-chain users
People who hold or use assets on more than one ecosystem and want one place to review balances, activity, and wallet actions.

### 3. DeFi and dApp users
Users who connect to decentralized applications, approve token usage, trade, bridge assets, or sign wallet requests.

### 4. Long-term holders
Users who mainly need visibility and safe handling of assets such as BTC, ETH, SOL, and related tokens.

### 5. NFT users
Collectors or users who want to view and transfer digital collectibles alongside their other assets.

## Product promise
**One wallet experience for managing multi-chain assets and Web3 actions—with user confirmation and control at every critical step.**

## Positioning statements
Use:
- “A self-custody wallet for managing assets across supported blockchain ecosystems.”
- “A unified place to view assets, review transactions, connect to Web3 services, and manage frequent addresses.”
- “A product designed to make multi-chain wallet activity easier to understand.”

Avoid:
- “The safest wallet.”
- “Guaranteed best bridge route.”
- “Zero-risk DeFi.”
- “Guaranteed investment gains.”

## Supported ecosystem language
The repository describes support around EVM-compatible networks, Solana, and Bitcoin. Use “supported networks” unless the current deployment list has been verified. Do not imply every chain, token, dApp, or bridge route is available to every user.
